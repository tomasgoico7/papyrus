# NNNN. Short imperative title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What made a decision necessary. The forces in play — constraints, costs,
deadlines, things that were already true and could not be changed. Written so
someone who was not there can see why the obvious answer was not obvious.

## Decision

What was chosen, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Consequences

What this buys, and what it costs. The costs are the point: a record listing only
benefits is advertising, not a decision. Name what would have to change for this
to be revisited.
